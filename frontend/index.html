Disclaimer of Liability

AnyPlay is exclusively an advanced web search application. It acts as a specialized web browser that aggregates search results and metadata from publicly available sources on the internet.

AnyPlay does not host, store, stream, or distribute any copyrighted media files. All media is provided by third-party external services and aggregators over which we have no control. Users are solely responsible for ensuring their usage complies with their local laws and regulations.
